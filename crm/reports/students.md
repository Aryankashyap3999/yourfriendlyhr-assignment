# Reports: Students

Native CRM reports against Students / Student Academic Enrollments,
filtered to the current academic year unless noted.

| Report | Source | Grouping/Filter |
|---|---|---|
| Total Students | Students | filter `Status = Active` |
| Students by Academic Year | Student Academic Enrollments | grouped by Academic Year |
| Students by Class | Student Academic Enrollments | filter current year, grouped by Class |
| Students by Section | Student Academic Enrollments | filter current year, grouped by Class + Section |

Dashboard: "Student Distribution" — grouped bar of current-year enrollment
by Class, drillable to Section.
